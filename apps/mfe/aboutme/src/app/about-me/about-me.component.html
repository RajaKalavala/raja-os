About Me

Principal Software Architect, system thinker, and builder of things that matter

I fell in love with building software the moment I realized code could turn ideas into reality. What started as tinkering with small scripts quickly evolved into an obsession with crafting elegant solutions. I spent countless nights debugging code, learning new frameworks, and building projects that nobody asked for — but that taught me everything.

Over the past 9+ years, I've evolved from a curious junior developer to a Principal Software Architect. I've worked across e-commerce, healthcare, and platform engineering — shipping products used by millions. Each role taught me that great software isn't just about code; it's about understanding people, processes, and problems.

"Great software is built by those who understand that simplicity is the ultimate sophistication."

I believe software engineering is a team sport. My role as an architect isn't to dictate solutions but to enable teams to build sustainable, scalable systems. I obsess over developer experience, clean APIs, and documentation that people actually read.

When I'm not architecting systems or reviewing PRs, you'll find me traveling to new places, playing football or badminton with friends, gaming, or exploring cozy coffee shops. I believe life is about balance — the same discipline I bring to coding, I bring to staying active and exploring the world. I'm a firm believer that the best engineers are perpetual students — there's always something new to learn, both in code and in life.
